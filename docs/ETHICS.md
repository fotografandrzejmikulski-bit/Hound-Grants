# Ethics Statement

## Research purpose

HOUND is designed to improve the measurement of procedural reliability in autonomous AI systems. It is not intended to demonstrate offensive capability against real targets or to provide operational instructions for bypassing safeguards.

## Synthetic-by-default principle

All benchmark environments should be synthetic, disposable, and isolated. Real customer data, credentials, financial accounts, production infrastructure, or personal information are prohibited from default benchmark scenarios.

## Human subjects and experts

Where experts contribute annotations, the project should document recruitment, compensation, conflicts of interest, confidentiality, and data handling. Personal information should not enter public scenario releases unless there is a specific lawful and ethical basis.

## Dual-use considerations

Cybersecurity and enterprise-authorization scenarios can have dual-use properties. The benchmark therefore emphasizes measurement, logging, policy adherence, and bounded simulation rather than operational exploitation.

## Fairness and attribution

The benchmark should avoid language that attributes human motives or malicious intent to models based solely on trajectories. Labels describe observable behavior under a defined scenario.

## Publication ethics

Results should include negative findings, methodological limitations, failed scenarios, evaluator disagreement, and changes introduced during development. Claims should be traceable to their evidence.
