# Reviewer Guide

## What can be evaluated now

This repository contains an executable foundation and a public synthetic scenario corpus. A reviewer can inspect the metric definitions, state model, event schema, evaluator logic, tests, CI configuration, and proposal methodology directly.

## What is proposed rather than completed

The large-scale 300-scenario study, expert calibration, held-out test set, model matrix, and statistically powered results are proposed research deliverables. They are not represented as completed empirical findings.

## Primary review questions

1. Is the research question falsifiable?
2. Are SR, PCR, CSR, and MG operationally well-defined?
3. Can the evaluator distinguish task failure from procedural failure?
4. Are model and scaffold effects separated?
5. Can the state and event trail be replayed or independently audited?
6. Are safety controls sufficient to prevent contact with real systems?
7. Does the empirical plan provide a credible test against relevant prior work?
8. Are uncertainty and evaluator disagreement reported?

## Evidence discipline

Any external numerical claim must be sourced before submission. The proposal intentionally prefers a smaller set of verifiable claims over an inflated literature narrative.
